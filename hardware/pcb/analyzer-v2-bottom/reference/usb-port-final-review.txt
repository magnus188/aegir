Result: no new blocking electrical pinout or obvious local current-path defect

Checked details:
- J901 rotation +90 degrees preserves A5=CC1 and B5=CC2. A6/B6 join D+;
  A7/B7 join D-. All four logical VBUS contacts, four USB grounds and shell
  stakes have the correct nets. SBU1/2 remain separately unconnected.
- U901 channels match the original circuit. Explicit 1-10, 2-9, 4-7, 5-6 copper
  links exist; the design correctly does not rely on internal connections to NC
  pads. Grounds 3/8 have nearby separate 0.6/0.3 mm vias, with local top/bottom
  ground pours and the extended internal GND plane.
- D901 remains on connector VBUS before the existing OVP/input-limiter circuit.
  Its local VBUS branch is about 1.60 mm long and 0.4 mm wide; its GND return
  to a plane via is 0.76 mm long and 0.3 mm wide, with direct surrounding copper.
  This is a short local shunt connection, not proof of system ESD immunity.
- Each physical VBUS land has a short 0.4 mm escape, then 0.6 mm copper and two
  0.3 mm drilled vias; the longer bottom-layer trunk is 1.0 mm wide. No thin
  internal trace replaces the main power path. This presents no obvious local
  bottleneck for the existing 1.4 A input ceiling. Hot-plug/drop/temperature
  measurements under full current remain part of original prototype validation.
- Bottom-layer VBUS under the connector is separated by the PCB dielectric.
  The two signal vias at (58.8,111.75) and (58.8,112.75) are ordinary 0.5/0.25 mm
  through vias, explicitly tented on both faces. The front soldermask export
  confirms no apertures at these vias. They are outside solder-paste lands;
  local via filling/capping is not required by this geometry. Preserve tenting
  in CAM and inspect soldermask coverage before mounting the metal connector.
  GCT's drawing does not specify a blanket under-body copper keepout; there is
  no exposed signal land deliberately placed under the metal body.

Remaining meaningful conditions:
- The J901 GND-land 25 um rearward offset is the documented fabrication
  adaptation, not a manufacturer-approved footprint. Maintain the narrow
  0.20 mm NPTH rule; measured local clearance is 0.2187 mm. No 0.15 mm waiver.
- Keep requested USB4105-GF-A. Its 0.95 mm shell stakes do not protrude through
  1.6 mm PCB; component-side/reflow soldering and shell-slot paste treatment
  must suit the assembly process. -120 would not remove this process requirement.
- CC/D fanout includes a few millimetres of pre-clamp routing and layer changes.
  Logical connectivity and short GND returns are correct, but only system ESD
  and USB-source qualification testing can validate actual transient behaviour.
- Existing hot-plug/OVP, charge-enable and enclosure qualification holds remain.

Mechanical height from primary GCT Rev B4 drawing, page 1 side view:
Mating centreline = 1.73 mm above PCB seating plane. With PCB top world Z22.1:
  opening centre world Z23.83 mm; body top world Z25.41 mm.

These are nominal dimensions; allow manufacturing/printing/plug tolerances and
provide local PCB support for insertion loads. This port has no waterproof rating.
