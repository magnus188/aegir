COORDINATE CONTRACT
Current native PCB origin is offset from the frozen mechanical contract:
  u = PCB x - 19.9; v = PCB y - 21.48491 (millimetres)
  world X = 50.4 + u = PCB x + 30.5
  world Y = 120 - v = 141.48491 - PCB y
  finished PCB back/front = world Z20.5/Z22.1 nominal.
Existing core remains 30 x 99 mm. Do not recenter the core when its outline
grows; preserve its exact two mount locations and the X/Y datum above.
H1 lower mount: native (45.5,113.48491), world (76,28).
H2 upper mount: world (54.8,114).

RECOMMENDED PCB ARRANGEMENT
Add material outside the existing +X edge (native x49.9), near the lower
USB/power area. Face USB4105 outward in +X, with footprint rotation +90 deg.
Keep the upper/lower extent unchanged so the board remains 99 mm long.
The parent designer's conservative tab candidate is:
  tab native x49.9..65, y103..120.48491
  resulting bounding size 45.1 x 99 mm
  connector footprint origin native (61.325,112), rotation +90 deg.
  connector mouth and footprint indicated PCB edge native x=65.
  connector signal-row native x57.645.
  connector courtyard native x56.565..65.505, y106.68..117.32.
This leaves 6.665 mm between the old core edge and connector courtyard for
the existing protection components, their local ground return and fanout.
The body/courtyard fits within the proposed tab's Y span. Keep the original
H1 screw/driver access clear; the connector is laterally outside that hole.
The old core's lower-region component bounds remain at or below x49.33;
none enters the new tab. Full copper DRC is the PCB designer's check.

A tighter option ends at native x=62 instead of 65 and places the socket
origin at 58.325. It would be 42.1 × 99 mm overall, with only 3.665 mm between
old core and connector courtyard. This is a mechanical option, not a
routed design: choose it only if the ESD placement and safe fanout remain
comfortable. Do not save 3 mm at the expense of routing or protection.

The connector footprint mouth is at local y=+3.675, its contact row at
y=-3.68, its body width 8.94 mm and body depth 7.35 mm. Do not infer a nose
overhang from another GCT socket: this library's suggested PCB edge is at
the front face. Use the manufacturer drawing and the selected plug when
designing the wall recess, clearance opening and final standoff.

ENCLOSURE CONSEQUENCES
For the x=65 candidate, connector front face is world X95.5 and port centre
is world Y29.48491. This is outside the current 85 mm-wide housing. A local
side bulge around the lower USB tab is the smallest change to the rest of
the case: preserve the front/display and the main core's current location.
Do not just increase CaseWidth: existing expressions recenter the display,
move PCB/support datums and enlarge other dependent geometry. The tab and
its port need a separate extension datum or carefully revised expressions.
Do not carry over the old 4.6 mm PCB-to-outer-wall gap at the socket mouth;
that would recess the USB face excessively. Exact wall location depends on
the selected cable plug moulding and opening, and remains to be fitted.

Remove the old bottom USB cartridge and bridge, close its opening, and
replace them with a supported side port. Provide a PCB underside ledge or
equivalent load path near the socket and positive strain/load support so
plug insertion does not rely on the tab flexing and solder alone. Preserve
access to the two original PCB screws and rear removal of the board.
The original narrow carrier also needs the new tab's support and local
clearance for soldered shell stakes. Verify stake depth and assembly
method against the exact GF-A drawing and the 1.6 mm board.

The protected holder occupied envelope remains world X5.4..47.4,
Y27..107.4,Z16.5..36.85, so the outward tab does not enter it. The display's
retained baseline outer depth ends around world Z14.1, ahead of the PCB;
the new connector is outward of its existing X envelope as well. These
separations do not prove fit of the changed enclosure/supports or cables.
The socket is not an IP-rated sealed panel connector; no waterproof claim
is established by this integration or a printed cover.

WHY NOT RETAIN THE OLD BOTTOM PORT POSITION
The main-board lower edge is world Y21. The old USB nose is near Y0.61,
and its centre is X42.5, left of the main core. A bottom extension to that
position would add roughly 20 mm length and a leftward dogleg. It would
increase the PCB beyond 100 mm and require a larger change to its outline.
The side tab retains the original 99 mm length and core layout.
