Required casing work

  2. Make the new side opening with clearance for the chosen cable body.
     Do not reuse the old 4.6 mm board-edge-to-case-wall gap at this socket;

  3. Close the old bottom USB opening and remove its cartridge/bridge.
  4. Rework the carrier locally for the tab while preserving its original
     core supports, rear-component openings and protected holder position.
